Measurements — the numbers the layout constants come from

    

      
Head: the px the clef + meter need against HEAD_PX, the clef alone against CLEF_PX, the meter alone against METER_PX, VexFlow's Stave.padding against BAR_PAD; from pixels, how far a flam and a drag reach before their note against FLAM_PX and DRAG_PX. Band: from pixels, the ink above the staff of every kind of event, bare and marked, alone and under a bracket, against INK_ABOVE; the labels against LABEL_INK_ABOVE; the sticking letters against STICKING_INK, which STAFF_BELOW is set from; then every figure and library piece against its own band (rowBand): no ink out of it, and under the letters at least STICKING_AIR times the air over them; the worst case's rows stacked as the app stacks them, one band apart (the pink lines). Highlight: the ink of every head and rest, each drawn alone, from pixels, against HEAD_INK and REST_INK — what the highlight box frames. Edges: every row of every figure and library piece against its own width — no ink out of it, none of it blank on the sides (BARLINE_OVERHANG). Colors: every row of every figure, library piece and band probe, engraved as the app engraves it, carries no colour but currentColor — the hook the theme paints through (themes.css, score.css); any other fill or stroke is listed with the row it was first seen on. Longest piece: ms per engraved row (the spec's line is 16 ms on the iPad). Motion: 30 s at 120 bpm, rAF intervals above 20 ms, scroll and pages.
